## A useful first conversation

Tell us, in broad terms:

- Which process or department would you like to improve?
- Where do time, quality or unclear responsibilities create friction today?
- Who owns the result, and what would count as an improvement?

Please do not send passwords, API keys, personal customer records or confidential documents with an initial enquiry. We can agree on a suitable data-sharing process later.

## Proposed pilot structure

| Step | Deliverable |
| --- | --- |
| Understand | Process scope, accountable owner and baseline measurement plan. |
| Design | Today / target organisation and explicit human decisions. |
| Configure | A supported workflow and the connections needed for that use case. |
| Review | Results reviewed against agreed quality criteria. |
| Decide | An evidence-based recommendation to continue, change or stop. |

Timing, scope and price are agreed individually. A conversation does not automatically create a paid engagement. No percentage saving or ROI is guaranteed.

## Current access

c9n is a local prototype with a guided-pilot deployment path. There is no public self-service hosted demo at present. Do not use the screenshots as evidence of production readiness for your particular process.

Use the [pilot brief](../templates/pilot-brief.md) to prepare privately. For a general, non-confidential product question, use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/dajor/c9n/discussions).
