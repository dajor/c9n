One process. Shared tasks.

A process connects individual tasks to a shared goal. For each task, you define the result you need, the inputs available, and who handles the work, review and approval.

Agents prepare results. People add missing information, assess quality and decide the next step. At an approval step, the illustrated process waits for the assigned person. If a result needs rework, the task goes back with clear feedback and must be reviewed again.

Example: from a request to an approved result

  1. WatcherCheck the input
  2. AgentDo the task
  3. ScriptCreate the export
  4. TestTest the result
  5. HumanApprove

Work stays in one shared workflow: tasks and results are handed over, decisions stay traceable, and your team retains responsibility.

SELF-HOSTED INSTALLATION · LOCAL PROCESSING

Your processes and data.
In your own environment.

Your processes connect confidential documents, internal rules and human decisions. In fully local operation, documents, tasks and results are processed and stored in your c9n installation. With models on your own computer or network, AI processing also stays there.

Local: your work
Processes, human approvals, the agent runtime, data storage and model management belong to the planned local c9n package.
Cloud: supporting services
The website and downloads, contact delivery and optional sign-in to external providers. These services process connection, sign-in or contact data.

External models and services can be explicitly connected. The required content is then also processed outside your installation. Fully local processing requires local models and a workflow without those external services.

Target architecture: the shared package with Hermes and GPU management is being built.

PEOPLE · AI · COST PER TASK

What does a task
really cost?

In workflows handled entirely by people, work and rework often spread across inboxes, spreadsheets and tools. In workflows built entirely around AI, questions, reviews and approvals can be overlooked. What matters is the effort needed for a finished, reviewed result.

Teams and public administrations think in processes: from the initial request to a result someone takes responsibility for. c9n is being built around connecting human tasks, agent steps and approvals. Task costs include human work, review and rework as well as AI, infrastructure and allocated tool costs. Waiting time also contributes to total turnaround time.
