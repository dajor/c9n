Five actors. Clear responsibility.

Not every automated task needs AI. Each actor has a different job in the collaboration – and your team stays in charge of the decision.

Fink

Agent

Works on a task using an AI model, such as summarizing information or writing a draft.

One process. Shared tasks.

A process connects individual tasks to a shared goal. For each task, you define the result you need, the knowledge available, and who handles the work, review and approval.

Agents prepare results. People add missing information, assess quality and decide the next step. If a result needs rework, the task goes back with clear feedback.

Example: from a request to an approved result

  1. WatcherCheck the input
  2. AgentDo the task
  3. ScriptCreate the export
  4. TestTest the result
  5. HumanApprove

Work stays in one shared workflow: tasks and results are handed over, decisions stay traceable, and your team retains responsibility.

The figures show actor types and example tasks, not live jobs or real customer data.
