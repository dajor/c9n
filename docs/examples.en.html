EXAMPLES / PROCESSES

Three processes.
Agents do the work.
People decide.

Three example processes show how watchers, agents, scripts, tests and people work together in one flow. Each one states where a human decides and which paths exist for rework or rejection.

Answer a customer request

  1. WatcherCheck the input
  2. AgentAnalyse the request
  3. AgentDraft a reply
  4. HumanReview and approve
  5. ScriptHand over the result
Goal
Answer a request completely and traceably, without it getting stuck between mailboxes.
Who works
The watcher notices the new input and wakes the agent. The agent summarises the request, names open questions and drafts the reply. A script hands over the approved result.
Where the human decides
The assigned person reviews the draft. Without their explicit approval the process does not continue.
Paths
Approve: continue with the result. Rework: the agent receives feedback, revises, and the human reviews again. Reject: stop, clarify or follow the defined path.
What you measure
Human time for editing, review and rework, the number of returns, and turnaround time including waiting.

Review and approve expenses

  1. ScriptCapture the request
  2. TestAmount over €1,000?
  3. HumanReview the expense
  4. HumanRevise the amount
  5. ScriptClose the case
Goal
Review, revise and traceably approve expenses depending on the amount.
Who works
A script captures the request. A fixed check compares the amount with the limit. Neither needs AI.
Where the human decides
If the amount is above the limit, a person reviews need and amount. Up to the limit, the process closes without human review.
Paths
Approve: case closed. Reject: case rejected. Send back for rework: the amount is restated and goes through the check again.
What you measure
Share of cases that need human review, number of revisions and time to closure.

Create, test and approve an export

  1. WatcherCheck the input
  2. AgentDo the task
  3. ScriptCreate the export
  4. TestTest the result
  5. HumanApprove
Goal
Prepare a result so it is tested and only handed on after human approval.
Who works
All five actor types work together: the watcher notices the input, the agent does the task, a script builds the export package and a test checks it against fixed criteria.
Where the human decides
The test ends with “passed” or “failed”. Only then does the assigned person decide whether the tested result is approved.
Paths
Passed and approved: hand over the result. Failed or sent back: the task goes back with clear feedback and is reviewed again.
What you measure
Time per step, number of failed tests and returns, and the effort until an approved result.

Development status: the examples explain the setup and do not run a process. The new process execution with binding approval pauses is being built; supported steps are checked per test build.
