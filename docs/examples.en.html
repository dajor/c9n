EXAMPLES / PROCESSES

Three processes.
Agents do the work.
People decide.

Three example processes show how you split tasks between agents, fixed scripts and people. Each example states plainly what exists today and what is still open.

Design a better customer handover

  1. WatcherCheck the input
  2. AgentFind missing information
  3. AgentDraft a response
  4. HumanDecide
  5. ScriptHand over and record the outcome
The problem
Requests are passed between teams without a clear owner, complete information or a consistent response standard.
Human responsibility
Make commitments, handle exceptions and own the customer relationship.
What exists
Organisational design, role explanations, project links and workflow editors can be used to agree on this process. Specific runtime and external mailbox/CRM integrations must be assessed for each pilot.
Status
A proposed pilot blueprint, not a claim of a generally deployable end-to-end CRM automation.
What to measure
Human effort per request, number of handovers, avoidable rework, quality and clearly assigned responsibility before work starts.

From scattered ideas to reviewed knowledge

  1. ScriptCapture a note or text PDF
  2. AgentOrganise it
  3. HumanReview the suggestion
  4. ScriptStore the approved result
  5. AgentCreate a follow-up task or briefing
The problem
Useful notes and feedback are collected, then disappear into separate tools. People cannot tell which source supports a decision.
Human responsibility
Check the source, correct the summary and decide where the information belongs. AI output is a proposal until reviewed.
What exists
A bounded knowledge workflow, source versions, private-by-default entries, explicit sharing, human review and follow-up tasks. Automated organisation requires a configured supported model; manual organisation can be tried without one.
What is still open
General video/audio import, OCR, embeddings and broad multi-source retrieval.
What to measure
Review effort, corrections, source reuse and completed follow-up tasks. Do not equate the time a task waits in a queue with human working time.

From a brief to approved content

  1. ScriptGather approved facts
  2. AgentPrepare text and image variants
  3. HumanCompare and select
  4. HumanReview the exact version
  5. ScriptExport the approved result
The problem
A team produces more drafts but spends more time finding facts, comparing variants and correcting claims.
Human responsibility
Own the brief, check claims and image suitability, choose the strongest variant and approve publication separately.
What exists
A creative workspace with variant comparison, selection, versioned review and export. Model connections must be configured.
Current boundary
The broad process includes steps whose execution or external connector is still open. Export is not automatic social-channel publication.
What to measure
Time to an approved asset, human correction effort and first-review quality. Later campaign outcomes need actual channel data and an attribution method.
