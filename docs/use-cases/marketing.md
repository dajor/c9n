# From a brief to approved content

**Problem:** A team produces more drafts but spends more time finding facts, comparing variants and correcting claims.

**Journey:** Gather approved product and brand facts → prepare text/image variants → compare and select → review the exact version → export the approved result.

**Human responsibility:** Own the brief, check claims and image suitability, choose the strongest variant and approve publication separately.

**What exists:** A creative workspace with variant comparison, selection, versioned review and export. Model connections must be configured. An Advanced marketing graph can express the broader process.

**Boundary:** The broad marketing graph includes steps whose execution or external connector is still open. It is not proof of a completely automated campaign, and export is not automatic social-channel publication.

**What to measure:** Time to an approved asset, human correction effort and first-review quality. Later campaign outcomes need actual channel data and an attribution method.
